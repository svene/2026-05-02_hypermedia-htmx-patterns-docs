For reusable UI parts and components.

## 1. Parent Child

The parent fragment builds a chunk of slot markup (interpolating its own
`greeting` parameter) and passes it **down** into the child fragment as a
`gg.jte.Content` parameter. Use when a child owns a large chunk of HTML and the
parent only customises a small part of it.

## 2. Forwarder

A fragment that, from its parameter, decides whether to render itself or
delegate to another fragment, using JTE's native `@if`/`@else`.
